iron

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-12

Summary

iron is a workout tracker developed by Jacopo Dardini. No account is required. The Android app stores your training logs on your device and does not transmit them to the developer. There are no ads, analytics trackers, or cloud accounts.

Data on your device

iron stores workout sessions, exercises, sets, cardio entries, workout categories, templates, drafts, preferences, and rest timer state locally. These records power logging, history, progress charts, and your consistency grid.

Export and import

Settings lets you export training logs as a JSON file and import iron JSON exports. Export files contain your workout records in plain text and are not encrypted by iron. If you share an export through another app or save it to a cloud provider, you choose that destination and its privacy policy applies. Templates, drafts, and preferences are not included in training log exports. Android automatic app backup and device transfer are disabled for this release.

Template sharing

Templates can be shared separately as plain-text JSON files. They contain the template name, workout category, exercise names, set counts, rep targets, bodyweight flags, and an optional source-template name. They do not include logged weights or workout history. You choose where to save or send them. Imported templates are validated and processed on your device, and saved as a new copy after you review them.

Timer permissions

Notifications show your rest countdown on the lock screen and in the notification shade. Optional alarms permission allows an on-time completion alert while the phone sleeps. Without it, Android may delay the alert. Timer notifications contain only timer information. You can change these permissions in Android settings; no remote push service is used.

Sharing and support

iron does not sell your data or transmit your training history to the developer or advertisers. If you email support, the developer receives the message and any information or files you choose to include. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and retained only as needed to resolve it.

Retention and deletion

Your local records remain until you delete them. Delete workouts in the app, or clear app storage in Android settings to remove all local data. Uninstalling also removes app-local records. Exported files are separate copies: delete them from the locations where you saved or shared them. Contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence.
